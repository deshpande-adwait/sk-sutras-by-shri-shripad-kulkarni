## 2. Rule 2 Implementation: Centralisation of the Four Foundational Statements

### 2.1 The Four Foundational Statements Consolidated into Front Matter
Prior to this refinement, the following four statements appeared verbatim in all ten chapters:
1. **उद्योग महाग्रंथ विचार**: उद्यमेन हि सिध्यन्ति कार्याणि न मनोरथैः। कौशलं धृतिः शक्तिः सहकार्यं च राष्ट्रवर्धनम्।
2. **नीतिपूर्ण व्यापार व आचारसंहिता**: धर्मसंमतः अर्थलाभः। लोककल्याणार्थं वाणिज्यम्। निष्कामभावेन उद्योगपरिपालनम्।
3. **स्वावलंबन व संकटात संयम**: स्वावलम्बनं परमं सुखम्। आत्मसंयमः संकटे धैर्यम्।
4. **संत तुकाराम महाराज विचार**: 'जोडोनियां धन उत्तम व्यवहारे । उदास विचारे वेच करी ॥'

### 2.2 Central Placement & Exposition
These four statements have been moved and consolidated into a dedicated, prominent presentation box within Front Matter Section:

under the title:  
**`॥ चार मूलभूत ग्रंथीय अधिष्ठाने (The Four Common Foundational Scriptural Anchors) ॥`**

In this central section, each of the four statements is presented with:
- Sacred Title and Thematic Anchor

- Comprehensive Marathi Exposition & Enterprise Significance
- Professional English Translation & Governance Implication

## 3. Rule 1 Implementation: Intelligent Handling of Repeated IKS Verses

### 3.1 First Appearance vs. Subsequent Appearance Protocol
Across the ten chapters, eight Indian Knowledge System (IKS) scriptural verses serve as secondary anchors. Under Rule 1:
- **First Appearance**: When a verse appears for the first time in the manuscript, its full scholarly apparatus is provided: Canonical Tradition, Exact Citation, Sanskrit Shloka, Marathi Translation, English Translation, and Enterprise Principle.
- **Subsequent Appearances**: When the same verse reappears in later chapters to ground a different Udyoga Sutra, the verse text and canonical citation are **retained** (preserving independent understandability), but the duplicate multi-paragraph translations are replaced by a **concise chapter-specific nuance (`प्रकरणाशी संबंधित विशेष पैलू`)** and a **direct cross-reference** to the chapter where the complete exposition was first presented.

## 4. Specific Issues Resolved

1. **Misplaced Translation in Chapter 3 & Adjacent Sutras**:
   - In earlier drafts, translations or new sutra formulations appeared before the foundational source texts, causing severe reader confusion.
   - **Resolution**: Implemented strict hierarchical flow where Section 1 (Foundational Sources) must be completely presented, translated, and harmonized before Section 2 (Bridge) and Section 3 (New Sutra) appear.
2. **Missing DOs and DON'Ts Framework**:
   - Earlier drafts lumped practical enterprise guidance into a generic bulleted list without clear delineation of constructive actions vs. critical pitfalls.
   - **Resolution**: Created Section 6 featuring dedicated, color-coded subsections: **काय करावे (DOs)** in light green and **काय टाळावे (DON'Ts)** in light red, with role-specific guidance.
3. **Cryptic Formatting & Technical Jargon**:
   - Internal document codes (`DOC-01`, `DOC-02`, etc.) and agent telemetry references were completely removed from the client-facing document.
   - **Resolution**: Classical texts are now introduced with their full dignified Sanskrit names (e.g., उद्योग महाग्रंथ, कौटिलीय अर्थशास्त्र, भर्तृहरी नीतिशतक).
4. **Devanagari Transliteration Standardisation**:
   - Added precise IAST transliterations for all 20 source shlokas and all 10 new Udyoga Sutras to aid non-Devanagari scholars and international readers.

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The **SK Sutras** project utilizes an autonomous three-agent architecture designed to synthesize an authoritative comparative treatise between the **84 Narada Bhakti Sutras** and the **100 Shandilya Bhakti Sutras**, producing the unified **Udyoga Sutra** manuscript in classical Marathi and Devanagari Sanskrit.

This document delivers an exhaustive capability, linguistic, and financial analysis of the leading commercial frontier Large Language Models (LLMs). The financial modeling is constructed under a **100% client pass-through billing model**—meaning overall cost is secondary to **absolute output quality, classical Sanskrit fidelity, high-register Marathi eloquence, and zero doctrinal hallucinations**.
